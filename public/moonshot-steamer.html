What was wired
Steamer method → Moonshot field

Weighted recent results + regression to the mean (Cross / Davidson / Rosenbloom). Amount of regression varies by component and sample — not Marcel’s fixed pad. Moonshot already shrinks batter HR/PA toward league with a 140-PA prior and pitcher HR/BF with 220 BF. The Regress w column is PA / (PA + 140): Steamer’s idea of “how much of this is the player vs the league.”

Recency is Moonshot factors.form (last-10 barrels, tanks, pull-air, trend). Steamer uses year-weights that decay; we only have this season + 10 games on the board, so form is the honest recency term.

Park-adjusted vs park-neutral is a real Steamer split (pn=0/1). Park-neutral P strips tonight’s park multiplier off pHrPa and rebuilds P(at least one) vs starter TBF. Posted P keeps the park.

Platoon vL/vR is Steamer’s split product. Moonshot factors.platoon already blends season vs-L/R with week barrels vs that hand.

Playing time / role: Steamer projects season PA (or Steamer600 = 600 PA). Moonshot splits starter PA vs full-game PA. Full-game sketch uses the same per-PA rate over gamePa — it is not the published number and does not include the pen’s true talent.

Not wired (we do not have it): Steamer aging curves, MLE/minor-league translations, Steamer’s own playing-time model, pitch-tracking pitcher engine, percentiles, or the FanGraphs feed. This view does not fetch or copy Steamer output.

Posted pHr is unchanged. No math retune.
